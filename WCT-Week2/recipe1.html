Fried Chicken Recipe

Crispy and Juicy Homemade Fried Chicken

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken pieces
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Wash and clean the chicken
  2. Season with salt and pepper
  3. Mix egg and milk in a bowl
  4. Dip chicken into the mixture
  5. Coat the chicken with flour
  6. Heat oil in a pan
  7. Fry chicken until golden brown
  8. Remove and serve hot
